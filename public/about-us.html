About MEI Drive Africa

We turn driving knowledge into real driving skills, for first-time learners, everyday motorists, professional drivers and fleets across Africa.

Good driving is more than controlling a vehicle. It involves observation, anticipation, patience, knowledge, judgement and respect for other road users.

MEI Drive Africa offers structured driver training and exam preparation, alongside the Road Journal, our library of practical articles on road safety, vehicle knowledge and everyday driving in Kenya.

Whether you are preparing for your first lesson, working as a professional driver or learning about electric vehicles, our goal is to make useful information accessible and practical.
